## 9 讲主题

1. Course Overview — 课程概览
2. Test-Time Compute Scaling — 测试时计算扩展
3. Robust Verification — 鲁棒验证
4. Learning from Feedback with Tools/Code — 工具与代码反馈学习
5. Planning and Multi-Step Reasoning — 规划与多步推理
6. Train Time Scaling / Scaling RL — 训练时扩展与强化学习
7. Self-Improvement and Deep Research Agents — 自我改进与深度研究智能体
8. Agentic Evaluations and Long Horizon Tasks — 智能体评估与长时程任务
9. Future Research Areas — 未来研究方向
